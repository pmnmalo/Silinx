In short:
- The Circuit editor has a servo (SG90): an Arduino's or an FPGA's PWM moves its arm, in Try it, in the System Emulator and in the circuit's HDL (#5347).

In detail:
- A servo SG90 on PWM as a circuit's part (#5347, part of #3902; co-design's peripherals): `core/circuit-servo.js` (its 3 wires: GND, VCC, its signal; each pulse measured from its rise to its fall in the simulation's time, its width the angle it asks for, 0.5 ms 0° to 2.5 ms 180°, a whole degree, its ends beyond; its arm moving at 60° in 0.1 s; a width or a period that is no servo's said once; unpowered: still). Simulated live (`core/circuit-sim.js`: `servos()`, `servoWarnings()`), drawn with its arm at its angle (`web/js/circuit-draw-servo.js`) in the Circuit editor's Try it and the System Emulator's circuit nodes, its warnings in the editor's status and a System's run; its checks (powered; its signal driven by something, a board's pin counting: its program's or its design's); its Part info, its row in the Parts panel's Outputs. In the HDL an instance of its own entity (`core/circuit-hdl-servo.js`, simulation only), each angle its pulses ask for said, in VHDL and in Verilog. The MCP's simulate_drawing (its arm each step) and a System's simulate (`circuit <node>: M1 at 135°, its last pulse 2 ms`; the boards' line). Not exported to KiCad. Portuguese labels. The user's guide, docs/CODESIGN.md. Tests: circuit-servo (the widths and angles, its motion, its warnings, unpowered; in a circuit, its pulses timed; its checks; its HDL with no warnings, its angles the live one's in VHDL and in Verilog; an Arduino's Timer1 fast PWM on D9 moving it to 135° through a System's circuit node), mcp-circuit (simulate_drawing's arm, check_drawing), web-i18n (its message), the UI's circuit-servo (placed from the Outputs, its arm, Check, Part info, Portuguese; the System Emulator's arm at 135°; both interfaces), circuit-coverage, kicad-library, circuit-parts and circuit-parts-panel (the Outputs).
