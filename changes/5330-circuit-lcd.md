In short:
- The Circuit editor has a character LCD 16×2 (HD44780): an Arduino or switches write to it in Try it, in a co-design System and in the circuit's HDL (#5330).

In detail:
- A character LCD 16×2 with an HD44780 controller as a circuit's part (#5330, part of #3902, co-design's peripherals): `core/circuit-lcd.js` (its 16 pins in a row; its controller as the datasheet says it: a write on E's falling edge, RS a command or a character, clear, return home, entry mode with its decrement and shift, display on/off with its cursor and blink, cursor and display shifts, function set in 8-bit or 4-bit mode (two nibbles, as LiquidCrystal starts it) with one or two lines, the DDRAM's lines at 0x00 and 0x40, the CGRAM addressed; off at power-on, reset while unpowered; the A00 ROM's characters), simulated live (`core/circuit-sim.js`: `lcds()`), drawn with its glass and its two lines (`web/js/circuit-draw-lcd.js`) in the Circuit editor's Try it and the System Emulator's circuit nodes, its checks (not powered; RW not on GND; joined to nothing), its Part info, its row in the Parts panel's Outputs, its id LCD1, the status line's displays. In the HDL an instance of its own entity (`core/circuit-hdl-lcd.js`, simulation only), its controller as the live one's, its lines reported after each write in VHDL and in Verilog. The MCP's simulate_drawing (each step's LCD lines) and a System's simulate (its lines, `circuit <node>: LCD1 |…|`). Not exported to KiCad (its own). The editor's Fit and print count a part's body larger than its pins (`partCorners`: the LCD's, the boards' of #5215). Portuguese labels. The user's guide, docs/CODESIGN.md. Tests: circuit-lcd (each command against the datasheet in 8- and 4-bit mode, the shifts, the CGRAM, one line, power; its checks; its HDL compiled with no warnings and simulated in VHDL and in Verilog, its lines the live simulation's; an Arduino writing "Hi" through a System's circuit node; its corners), the UI's circuit-lcd (switches clicked in Try it writing an A, the glass lit, Fit showing the whole module, Check, Part info, Portuguese; both interfaces), circuit-coverage, kicad-library, mcp-circuit, circuit-parts and circuit-parts-panel (the Outputs).
