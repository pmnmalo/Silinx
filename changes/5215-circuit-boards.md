In short:
- The Circuit editor's Parts panel has Boards: the Arduino Uno and Nano, the Basys2 and the Nexys2, with their real pins; a board's 5V and GND power the breadboard (co-design, #5215).

In detail:
- The boards as a circuit's parts (#5215, co-design's phase 2): `core/circuit-boards.js` describes the Arduino Uno and Nano and the Basys2 and Nexys2, each as large as the board and with its real pins, from the boards' own descriptions (the System's MCU_BOARDS: D0…D13, A0…, the Uno's SDA and SCL joined to A4 and A5, the Nano a DIP-30 across a breadboard's channel, A6 and A7 inputs only; the FPGA boards' Pmod connectors from `core/boards.js`, each pin with its package pin, the Basys2's JA2 and JA3 its UART's). A board's 5V, 3V3 and GND are a supply's terminals (`supplyPins`, `isSupplyPin`: Try it, the checks, the HDL in VHDL and Verilog, the digital model); its other pins drive nothing in the circuit alone. The Parts panel's Boards group, the board drawn under the wires with its pins' names (`web/js/circuit-draw-board.js`), its Part info (its pins' roles: the board's pin, supply 3.3 V; an FPGA pin's package pin), its id (A1, F1), circuit_parts' boards (their System board, each pin's package pin and level); not exported to KiCad. Co-design's, so not in a release yet (#5202): the Boards group hidden, circuit_parts without them, edit_circuit refusing one. Portuguese labels. The user's guide (EN, PT), docs/CODESIGN.md. Tests: circuit-boards (the pins against MCU_BOARDS and the boards' Pmod resources, a Nano on a breadboard powering an LED, the HDL compiled, elaborated and simulated with no warnings in VHDL and in Verilog, a board alone, a short), the UI's circuit-boards (the Boards group, a Nano placed, wired, Check with no warnings, Try it's levels, Part info, Portuguese; both interfaces), codesign-release (a release: no Boards in the panel, circuit_parts without them, edit_circuit refusing one), circuit-coverage, circuit-part-info, kicad-library, mcp-circuit.
