- **The System Emulator's Explorer 16/32 redrawn after the real board (#5579):** web/js/emulator-explorer.js, after a

  jack, the slide potentiometer R6 under it, the PIM socket in the middle, the LEDs D3 to D10 above the buttons S3 to S6
  and MCLR along the bottom edge, the temperature sensor, the USB connectors and the power jack on the left edge, the
  mikroBUS sockets A and B with their Pmod headers at the top right, the PICtail Plus headers and edge connector on the
  right; its colours the stylesheet's (web/css/system-editor.css, `--exp-*`).
