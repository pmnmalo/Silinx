In short:
- Emulate System draws a circuit node beside the boards: its LEDs light as it runs, its switches and buttons are clicked (#5322).

In detail:
- The System Emulator draws a co-design System's circuit nodes (#5322, after #5215): each its circuit as the Circuit editor draws it, beside the boards (web/js/system-emu-view.js), its LEDs and displays painted from the run, a click on a switch or a DIP switch's lever turning it, a button held down while the mouse is; core/system-emu.js's circuit boards (their inputs: a part's id, a DIP switch's lever `DIP1:2`; what they show: their lamps, their inputs, the lamps lit) and setBoardInput's circuit; the circuit node's setInput and its clocks ticking at their frequency (core/system-sim-circuit.js); the worker's set (core/system-host.js). The MCP's simulate takes a circuit's switches and buttons as controls and says its lamps lit (`<node>'s circuit: lit D2.`); its description of controls says so (tools/list within its budget, #4752). The user's guide (EN, PT), docs/CODESIGN.md. Tests: system-circuit (a circuit node emulated: its switch set, the FPGA's message, its LEDs, its clock; a clock's edges; a DIP switch's levers), mcp-system (controls on a circuit node, its line, the wrong ones refused), the UI's system-emu-circuit (the circuit drawn, S1 clicked pressing the FPGA's button, D2 as LD0, the console's lines, Portuguese; both interfaces).
