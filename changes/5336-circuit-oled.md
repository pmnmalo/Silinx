In short:
- The Circuit editor has a graphic OLED 128×64 (SSD1306) on I²C: an Arduino writes to it through a co-design System's circuit node, and Emulate shows its pixels (#5336).

In detail:
- A graphic OLED 128×64 with an SSD1306 controller on I²C as a circuit's part (#5336, part of #3902, co-design's peripherals): `core/circuit-i2c.js` (an I²C peripheral fed SCL's and SDA's changes: START, its address, each byte MSB first, its ACK pulling SDA low for the ninth clock, STOP; another address or a read not acknowledged), `core/circuit-oled.js` (its 4 pins, GND, VCC, SCL, SDA open drain; its controller as the datasheet says it: the control byte, Co and D/C#, the commands and their parameters, display on/off, the charge pump, the horizontal, vertical and page addressing modes, the column and page ranges, the page mode's page and column nibbles, contrast, inverse, entire display on, the segment remap, the COM scan direction, the start line; its GDDRAM 8 pages of 128 bytes; lit while on with its charge pump on; reset while unpowered), simulated live (`core/circuit-sim.js`: its ACK a driver of SDA, `oleds()`), drawn with its pixels (`web/js/circuit-draw-oled.js`) in the Circuit editor's Try it and the System Emulator's circuit nodes; its checks (joined to nothing; not powered; SCL or SDA with no pull-up); its Part info, its row in the Parts panel's Outputs, its id OLED1. An Arduino's TWI on A4 and A5 reaches it through a circuit node (#5215), its lines released (Z) and pulled up on the breadboard: the open drain already the node's. In the HDL an instance of its own entity (`core/circuit-hdl-oled.js`, simulation only: its SDA driven and read), its peripheral and controller as the live ones, what it shows reported after each STOP in VHDL and in Verilog with no lint warnings. The MCP's simulate_drawing (each step's OLED, its rows) and a System's simulate (`circuit <node>: OLED1 on, N pixel(s) lit`). Not exported to KiCad. Portuguese labels and Check messages. The user's guide, docs/CODESIGN.md. Tests: circuit-oled (the controller against the datasheet: modes, ranges, nibbles, Co commands, parameters, the mapping, the charge pump, reset; the I²C peripheral alone; in a circuit with a 7405's open collectors as the master: the ACKs, another address not, the pixels, no short, power off; its checks; its HDL with no warnings, its reports the live ones' in VHDL and in Verilog; an Arduino's TWI writing to it through a System's circuit node, its report line, no warnings), the UI's circuit-oled (Emulate: the Uno writing, its pixels lit; its row, Check, Part info, Portuguese; both interfaces), web-i18n, circuit-coverage, kicad-library, mcp-circuit, circuit-parts and circuit-parts-panel (the Outputs).
