In short:
- The Circuit editor has a temperature sensor LM75 on I²C: its temperature set while trying it (− and +), read by an Arduino in a co-design System, its OS output over its limit (#5342).

In detail:
- A temperature sensor LM75 on I²C as a circuit's part (#5342, part of #3902; co-design's peripherals): `core/circuit-lm75.js` (a DIP-8: SDA, SCL, OS, GND, A2, A1, A0, VCC; its registers as the datasheet says them: the pointer, the temperature in 9 bits of 0.5 °C, the configuration's shutdown, mode, OS polarity and fault queue, THYST 75 °C and TOS 80 °C at power-on; OS in comparator and in interrupt mode); the I²C peripheral reads (`core/circuit-i2c.js`: each byte sent bit by bit as SCL falls, the master's ACK the next one, its NACK the end; its address a function of the address pins). Simulated live (`core/circuit-sim.js`: `sensors()`, `setTemp`), its saved temperature (the part's `temp`, its Properties' Temperature, edit_circuit's set), drawn with its temperature between a − and a + clicked while trying it (`web/js/circuit-draw-lm75.js`), in the Circuit editor and the System Emulator (its temperature a control of the circuit node); its checks (powered; SCL and SDA pulled up once they are joined to something: an unused line is no bus, the OLED's too). In the HDL an instance of its own entity (`core/circuit-hdl-lm75.js`, simulation only), its saved temperature its generic, each byte it sends said, in VHDL and in Verilog. The MCP's simulate_drawing (a step's temperature by the sensor's id; its temperature and OS), a System's simulate (a control's value in °C at its time; its line), edit_circuit's temp (its descriptions said shorter for tools/list's budget, #4752). Not exported to KiCad. Portuguese labels. The user's guide, docs/CODESIGN.md. Tests: circuit-lm75 (the registers, OS in both modes, the fault queue, polarity, shutdown; the peripheral's reads; in a circuit: its address, the bytes read, the ACKs; its temperature set while running, OS; its checks; its HDL with no warnings, its bytes the live one's in VHDL and in Verilog; an Arduino's TWI reading it through a System's circuit node), mcp-circuit (simulate_drawing's temperature, edit_circuit's temp), mcp-system (a control's temperature), the UI's circuit-lm75 (Try it's − and +, OS lighting an LED over TOS until under THYST, its Temperature, Portuguese; the System Emulator's +; both interfaces), circuit-coverage, kicad-library, circuit-parts and circuit-parts-panel (the Inputs).
