In short:
- A circuit can be a node of a co-design System: its boards are the System's Arduino and FPGA nodes, its wires between their pins the links, simulated with them (#5215).

In detail:
- A circuit as a System's node (#5215, co-design's phase 2, step 2): a node of kind `circuit` names a `.circ.json` of the project; its boards (the Arduino Uno and Nano, the Basys2 and Nexys2 of the Circuit editor) are the System's nodes their labels name, each board pin it wires a link (`core/system-circuit.js`): an Arduino's both ways, an FPGA's the port of its module on that package pin, the way it goes. The scheduler runs the circuit's own simulation as a node (`core/system-sim-circuit.js`, pins driven from outside: `createCircuitSim`'s `external` and `drive`; an AVR input's pull-up a weak 1), a wire of 1 µs each way; a run says each circuit's lamps and a short. Its check (`core/system-check-circuit.js`): its circuit given, a .circ.json of the project, no interfaces; a board naming no node or a node of another board; an FPGA pin on no port or an inout one; a pin both a link's and the circuit's; a 5 V Arduino pin and a 3.3 V FPGA input on one net; the nodes it reaches join something. The System editor's Circuit tool and its Properties' Circuit; the MCP's edit_system (a circuit node), check_system and simulate (its lamps, `circuits`). Portuguese labels. The user's guide (EN, PT), docs/CODESIGN.md. Tests: system-circuit (its pins, its problems, its check, its plan, a run between an Uno and the uart example's FPGA: the Uno's D2 lighting an LED and pressing the FPGA's button, its LD0 lighting another and read on D8, the pull-up's weak 1, a short; the adapter alone with a 7404; its edits), mcp-system (edit_system, check_system and simulate of a circuit node; its circuit not there), the UI's system-circuit (the Circuit tool, its Properties, Check, Try it's LED and console line, Portuguese; both interfaces).
