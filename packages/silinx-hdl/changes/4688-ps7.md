In detail:

- **The Zynq's PS7 and the BIBUF, UNISIM primitives (#4688):** src/unisim-ps7.js, added to UNISIM's models. The PS7's
  model has UG585's ports; its FCLK_CLK0 runs at FCLK0_PERIOD (50 MHz by default) and its reset is released after four
  periods, with M_AXI_GP0 idle (the PS's program is not simulated). The BIBUF does nothing. A Zynq PL top that
  instantiates them elaborates and simulates, and the synthesis passes the PS7 on as Yosys's cell.
